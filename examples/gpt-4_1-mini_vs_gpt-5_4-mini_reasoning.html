### Step 3: Thermal Conductivity Differences

- **Thermal conductivity** is a material property that measures how well heat is conducted through a material.
- Metals have **high thermal conductivity** (e.g., copper ~400 W/m·K, steel ~50 W/m·K).
- Wood has **low thermal conductivity** (typically around 0.1–0.2 W/m·K).

---

### Step 4: Heat Transfer Dynamics on Touch

- When you touch the metal spoon:
  - Heat from your skin rapidly flows into the spoon because metal conducts heat efficiently.
  - The metal near your skin quickly absorbs heat and also transfers it away into the rest of the spoon and the surrounding air.
  - This continuous heat flow **removes heat from your skin faster**, causing your skin temperature at the contact point to drop quickly.
- When you touch the wooden spoon:
  - Heat from your skin flows into the wood much more slowly because wood is a poor conductor.
  - The wood near your skin warms up slightly but does not transfer heat away quickly.
  - Less heat is drawn from your skin, so your skin temperature at the contact point drops less.

---

### Step 5: Sensory Perception of Temperature

- Your skin's thermoreceptors detect the **rate of heat loss**.
- A faster heat loss (as with metal) is interpreted as "colder."
- A slower heat loss (as with wood) is interpreted as "warmer" or "less cold."

---

1. **Both spoons at room temperature; skin warmer.**
2. **Touch initiates heat flow from skin to spoon.**
3. **Metal's high thermal conductivity allows rapid heat transfer away from skin contact point.**
4. **Wood's low thermal conductivity limits heat transfer.**
5. **Faster heat loss from skin to metal causes skin temperature to drop quickly.**
6. **Thermoreceptors sense rapid heat loss as cold sensation.**
7. **Therefore, metal spoon feels colder than wooden spoon at the same temperature.**

- The **heat capacity** of the spoon also matters but less so in this immediate sensation.
- The metal spoon's surface near your skin remains closer to room temperature because heat is quickly conducted away.
- The wooden spoon's surface near your skin warms up slightly, reducing the temperature gradient and heat flow.
